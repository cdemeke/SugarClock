On your phone

SugarClock stores clock identifiers, nicknames and connection/schema metadata locally. Saved settings and pending edits use device-only Keychain storage available when the phone is unlocked, without iCloud Keychain synchronization. An explicitly entered replacement secret can remain there until its update is confirmed or the change is discarded. Ordinary clock responses show whether secrets are configured instead of returning their contents. Bluetooth bonding keys are managed by the operating system.

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not implement a SugarClock sign-in. It does not read HealthKit or request your phone's location. Removing a saved clock removes its local settings workspace and metadata; it does not erase the clock or necessarily remove its system Bluetooth bond. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to erase Keychain items.

Between the app and your clock

Bluetooth transfers settings and commands to the selected clock. Pairing uses an on-clock passkey and platform-managed bonding. The clock persists configuration so it can operate independently of the app. Resetting Bluetooth bonds preserves clock configuration; factory reset is a separate action.

From the clock to online services

The clock uses its own Wi-Fi to access the configured glucose provider, optional weather service, firmware delivery and SugarClock fleet service. Credentials are used with the provider you select. Those providers' practices also apply.

Current fleet firmware reports a stable installation identifier, firmware version, operational state and enabled-feature categories. The reviewed telemetry omits glucose values, patient identifiers, credentials, Wi-Fi names and source URLs. It is pseudonymous device reporting, not anonymous reporting. The service sees a network address; approximate location lookup may be enabled by the operator. Current fleet firmware has no reporting opt-out. Retention, access and deletion procedures need the operator's confirmation.

Websites and support

External links open separate websites. The existing SugarClock website uses its own analytics configuration. This draft launch page loads no analytics or third-party scripts. Information you voluntarily send for support is also handled by the chosen support channel; avoid sending credentials or personal health data.

Your choices and requests

You can discard pending edits, remove saved clocks and reset clock Bluetooth bonds. Provider accounts are managed by their respective providers. A public contact and a verified process for fleet-data access/deletion requests must be added here before launch, together with the applicable retention and regional-rights information.
