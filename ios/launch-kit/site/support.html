A little help

Get back to your clock.

Before this becomes the App Store Support URL, add the publisher's public support email and any required business contact details. Do not substitute a tester's private email.

Clock not found

Check that Bluetooth is enabled and SugarClock has permission in iOS Settings. Keep the app open near the clock. For a new pairing, hold only the clock's middle button for three seconds and release. Older firmware may need a USB or supported OTA upgrade before it can appear in the app.

It is reconnecting

The clock may briefly pause Bluetooth for network operations. After a clock has loaded once, you can continue editing saved settings. Tap Update clock to connect and submit the reviewed changes. An unconfirmed update needs verification; avoid repeatedly submitting the same operation.

A replacement phone or stale pairing

On the clock, hold only the middle button for ten seconds, then release to reset Bluetooth bonds. Forget the old Bluetooth device in iOS Settings if present, then pair again. This resets all phone bonds on that clock while keeping Wi-Fi, source and display settings.

Wi-Fi connects, but readings do not arrive

Wi-Fi association, internet access and provider authentication are separate checks. Verify the provider credentials and that its account or URL has current readings. Keep using your primary glucose device while troubleshooting.

Sharing a problem

Include app and firmware versions, phone model, the action you tried, and any error wording. Remove passwords, source URLs, tokens, Wi-Fi names and personal glucose information from screenshots. Do not post credentials in public issues.
