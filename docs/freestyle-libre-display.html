FreeStyle Libre display via LibreLinkUp

Set up a SugarClock FreeStyle Libre display using a LibreLinkUp follower account, select the right person, and troubleshoot sharing and stale readings.

Before you startConnection stepsTroubleshootingReferences

Before you start

SugarClock (Sugar Clock) displays FreeStyle Libre readings received through LibreLinkUp. The clock uses an unofficial follower API over Wi-Fi; it does not scan or pair with a Libre sensor.

  • A Ulanzi TC001 running SugarClock and connected to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with internet access.
  • A supported Libre app and sensor combination that actually shares current readings to LibreLinkUp in your country. The project's FAQ lists Libre 2, 2 Plus, 3, and 3 Plus through LibreLinkUp; regional app and sharing availability still matter.
  • A LibreLinkUp follower account with an accepted sharing connection. First verify that the follower can see the intended person's current data in the official LibreLinkUp app.

Use the LibreLinkUp account's credentials. LibreLink, LibreView, and LibreLinkUp are different roles in this setup; the sensor user's login is not automatically a working follower login.

Connect LibreLinkUp to the clock

  1. In the source Libre app, open Connected Apps → LibreLinkUp and invite the follower's email address. Complete the invitation in LibreLinkUp. Labels can vary with the regional app.
  2. Sign in to the official LibreLinkUp app and complete any required account verification, terms, or privacy prompts. Confirm that the intended person's current readings are visible.
  3. Open the clock's local settings page using the IP address shown on the clock. In Settings → Blood Sugar, choose FreeStyle Libre (LibreLinkUp) as the source.
  4. Save the LibreLinkUp Email and LibreLinkUp Password, then use Test Connection. SugarClock detects the region automatically.
  5. One sharing person is selected automatically. If several people share with the account, select Person to display and save again.
  6. Compare the person, value, units, trend, and reading age with LibreLinkUp. Check the selection before relying on a glance at the display.

The clock remembers the person's stable ID across restarts. If that person stops sharing, it stops accepting readings rather than switching to someone else. Changing the Libre email clears the saved person and detected region; test and select again.

Troubleshooting

No people appear
Check the invitation email and the account used to accept it. The official LibreLinkUp app must show an active connection for that same follower account.
More than one person is listed
Choose the intended person explicitly and save. A successful account login alone does not complete this selection.
Account action or login rejection
Complete the requested action in the official app. Test Connection respects the same retry cooldown as background polling. Repeated presses do not bypass it; wait for the current cooldown, which can grow to an hour.
Login works but readings are rejected
The clock requires synchronized network time and a valid sensor timestamp no more than ten minutes old. Check the source app, internet access, and NTP over UDP 123.
Region or account was changed
Save the changed credentials, test, and select the person again. Keep the default polling interval while diagnosing the connection.

Your connection and settings

Configure account credentials only on your clock's local page, not on this website. Use a trusted local network and do not expose the settings page to the internet. See the installation reporting notice before installing.

Not a medical device

SugarClock is an unofficial DIY project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dexcom, Abbott, Nightscout, or Ulanzi. Readings can be delayed or missing, and the clock may not sound alerts when its data is stale. Do not use SugarClock for treatment decisions or as a replacement for your CGM's own alarms; always confirm with your CGM receiver, app, or blood glucose meter.
