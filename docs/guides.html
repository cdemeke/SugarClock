Install once, then connect your data

All three paths use the Ulanzi TC001, a USB-C data cable for installation, and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Start with the browser installer and read its installation reporting notice. The clock shows its local IP address after connecting; use that address to open its settings from the same network.

The source app or server must continue to receive fresh readings. A working clock cannot recover data that is missing upstream.

Not a medical device

SugarClock is an unofficial DIY project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dexcom, Abbott, Nightscout, or Ulanzi. Readings can be delayed or missing, and the clock may not sound alerts when its data is stale. Do not use SugarClock for treatment decisions or as a replacement for your CGM's own alarms; always confirm with your CGM receiver, app, or blood glucose meter.
