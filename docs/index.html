Why

What makes SugarClock different

Most CGM displays are expensive, locked down, or hard to customize. SugarClock is different: it is affordable, open, and made for everyday visibility.

Built-in installation reporting

SugarClock automatically sends its random installation ID, firmware version, activity and health, configured data source (Dexcom, Libre, custom or demo), and enabled features such as weather and display pet to the fleet service. The administrator can view individual installations and add nicknames. Your network address may be used to estimate city and country.

Reporting cannot be switched off. These linked records are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Fleet reports exclude glucose readings, account credentials, Wi-Fi names, and your configured weather location. Automatic updates follow the administrator’s stable rollout; selected test clocks may receive candidates.

Install SugarClock

Browser-based setup for the Ulanzi TC001 smart pixel clock.

These steps assume you already have a Ulanzi TC001 smart pixel clock. Need one first? Use the buying links above.

  1. Plug in the clock with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Install and choose the clock when your browser asks.
  3. Finish setup locally by following the prompt on the display.
You may hear beeping during installation. That is expected while the clock updates, and it stops when setup is complete.

SugarClock is not a medical device. Use your CGM receiver, app, or meter for medical decisions.
